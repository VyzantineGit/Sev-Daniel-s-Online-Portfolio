About Me

Hi there! I am Sev Daniel Abonete, but most of my friends call me "Sev". I was born in Tagbilaran City, Bohol, Philippines. However, I was raised and now currently spending most of my life in Mariveles, Dauis. I am 21 years old and currently taking up Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BSCpE) at the University of Bohol.

However, and as I like to tell to people, my education background has been extensive. I spent my entire Pre-School and Elementary life in Prodigy Discovery School and later moved on to Junior and Senior High School at Bohol Wisdom School. After that, I managed to pass and enroll at the ever-prestigious Bohol Island State University (BSCpE being my first program of choice) but ended up leaving due to personal and academic reasons.

Upon transferring to my current university, I tried to take up Bachelor in Culture and Arts Education, which was in a vastly different field from the SHS STEM strand I graduated from. I did not stay for long in that program and ended up taking a gap semester in order to find myself. Feeling reinvigorated, I decided to retake my first program (of which I was still very fond of despite the 2-year hiatus) and never looked back since. Despite going back to square-one, I have no regrets and am loving every moment of my new Computer Engineering journey.

Outside the academic life, I have a lot of hobbies and interests that keep me busy. First of which, I am a local musician. I have been playing the guitar for 10 years, but my foray into Bohol's local music scene only started in 2022. I am a core member of a local band called the "Friday Cleaners", in which I serve as the rhythm guitarist, backup vocalist and recording engineer. Still carrying my passions in culture, I became a probationary member of Kasing Sining Inc., Bohol's premier local and community-based theater group
